circle()

Draw an arc.

A circle is a round shape defined by the x, y and d parameters. x and y set the
location of its center. d sets its width and height (diameter). Every point on the circle's
edge is the same distance, 0.5 * d, from its center. 0.5 * d (half the diameter) is
the circle's radius. See ellipseMode() for other ways to set its position.

Examples

ㅤㅤcreateCanvas(100, 100);

ㅤㅤbackground(200);

ㅤㅤcircle(50, 50, 25);

ㅤㅤdescribe('A white circle with black outline in the middle of
a gray canvas.');
}
